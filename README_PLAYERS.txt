GAME CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOUSE CONTROLS:
- BETTING SPOTS (Table Felt):
  - Left-click on any of the three felt betting circles (Center Main Bet, 
    Left "Pairs", or Right "21+3") to add $5 to that bet.
  - Hold [Shift] while left-clicking any spot to quickly add $25.
  - Right-click directly on any betting spot to clear that specific bet back to $0.
- BANKROLL TRAY (Bottom-Right):
  - Left-click any bankroll chip pile ($5, $25, $100, $500, $1,000) to add that 
    denomination to your staged bet. Hover over piles for tactile 2.5D lift!
  - Right-click bankroll chip piles to deduct chips from your bet.
- ACTION BUTTONS (Mahogany Rail):
  - Click "Deal Hand" to commit your bets and begin the round.
  - Click "Hit", "Stand", "Double", "Split", or "Surrender" as desired.
  - Click "Clear Bet" to reset all bets (main and side bets) back to $0.
  - Click "All In" to wager your entire bankroll on the main hand in Casino Mode.
- TOP BAR CONTROLS:
  - Click "[M] Sound" to toggle audio ON or OFF.
  - Click "[🔍 2x]" / "[🔍 1x]" to toggle 1x / 2x interface scaling.
  - Click "[⛶ Full]" / "[🗗 Window]" to toggle fullscreen display mode.
  - Click "[?] Help" to open the visual keyboard shortcuts overlay.
  - Click "[⚙ Settings]" (or press [Shift+M]) to switch between Casual, Betting, 
    and Training modes or reset career save data.

- [Shift+M]          : Cycle Play Mode (Casual -> Betting -> Training)

CASINO SIDE BETS (PERFECT PAIRS & 21+3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Casino Mode, you can place optional side bets in the flanking felt circles 
before the cards are dealt. Side bets settle immediately upon the initial 4-card 
deal, paying out instant winnings before you play out your main blackjack hand!

1. PERFECT PAIRS (Left Spot - Pays on player's first 2 cards):
   - Mixed Pair   (6:1)  : Same rank, different color (e.g., 8 of Spades + 8 of Hearts)
   - Colored Pair (12:1) : Same rank, same color, different suit (e.g., 8 of Spades + 8 of Clubs)
   - Perfect Pair (25:1) : Identical cards of same rank and suit (e.g., 8 of Spades + 8 of Spades)

2. 21+3 (Right Spot - 3-Card Poker hand combining Player 2 cards + Dealer Upcard):
   - Flush          (5:1)   : 3 cards of the same suit
   - Straight       (10:1)  : 3 cards of consecutive ranks (e.g., 5-6-7 or Q-K-A)
   - Three of a Kind(30:1)  : 3 cards of the same rank in different suits
   - Straight Flush (40:1)  : 3 consecutive cards of the same suit
   - Suited Trips   (100:1) : 3 identical cards of the exact same rank and suit!

Side Bet Lifecycles:
Side bets are strictly single-round wagers. Winning profits are credited to your 
bankroll immediately with celebratory floating badges, and losing chips are swept away. 
Side bets reset to $0 each hand so you are always in total control of your wager.
