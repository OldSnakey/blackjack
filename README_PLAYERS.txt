HOW TO PLAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Simply double-click "Blackjack.exe" to launch the game.
2. If Windows displays a "Windows protected your PC" notification:
   - Click "More info"
   - Click "Run anyway"
   (This is a standard one-time prompt for independent games not purchased 
   through the Microsoft Store).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOUSE CONTROLS:
- Click on chips ($5, $25, $100, $500) to place your bet.
- Click "Deal" to begin the round.
- Click "Hit", "Stand", "Double", "Split", or "Surrender" as desired.
- Click "All In" to wager your entire bankroll in Casino Mode.
- Click the sound icon in the top right to toggle audio ON or OFF.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:
- [Enter] or [Space] : Deal new hand / Hit
- [S]                : Stand
- [D]                : Double Down
- [P]                : Split Pair
- [R]                : Surrender
- [M]                : Mute / Unmute Audio Toggle
- [C]                : Clear Staged Bet (before deal)
- [A]                : All In

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME MODES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- CASINO MODE (Chips ON):
  Play with authentic casino bankroll rules ($1,000 starting bankroll), 
  staged betting, chip denominations, 3:2 Natural Blackjack payouts, 
  Insurance side bets, and automatic rebuy if you bust your bankroll.

- CASUAL MODE (Chips OFF):
  Zero-stress play with no bankroll overhead and instant deals. Perfect for 
  quick practice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Game won't open or closes immediately?
  Make sure you extracted the entire ZIP folder before running Blackjack.exe! 
  Do not run Blackjack.exe from inside the compressed ZIP window.
